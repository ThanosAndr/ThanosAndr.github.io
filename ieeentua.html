From 2015 to 2018 I volunteered with the IEEE Student Branch of NTUA, serving as Vice-Chair and Secretary (2015–2017). I was head organizer of technical talks, workshops, student projects and events, and represented the branch at events and congresses.

Notable Mentions:

  • LabDay 2016: Fund Raising Coordinator
  • LabDay 2017: Oversight Committee
  • 1st Hellenic TISP Day 2016: Main Speaker of Piraeus Event
  • IEEEXtreme 9.0 & 10.0 at NTUA: Head Organizer and Participant
