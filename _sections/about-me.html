I work best close to the hardware: making C++ fast, predictable and testable on embedded and real-time targets, from RTOS-based space processors to automotive ECUs. I enjoy turning fuzzy requirements into clear designs, and I've led small development teams as well as Klepsydra's work on EU- and ESA-funded projects, from proposals to technical requirements.

Away from the keyboard I like working with my hands. Woodworking is my favorite craft, and you'll often find me hiking in the countryside or behind a camera, trying to capture the beauty of nature.
