A 2016 project for the Sensors & Applications course at NTUA that combined hardware with cloud data. An Arduino Yún, connected through the Temboo service, fetched the outdoor temperature from Yahoo Weather and compared it with the indoor temperature measured by the board.

When the difference exceeded 10 °C, the Arduino switched off the air conditioner by sending the matching IR signal through a simple emitter. The aim was to avoid sudden temperature shocks when stepping outside. (The Yahoo Weather API has since been retired.)
